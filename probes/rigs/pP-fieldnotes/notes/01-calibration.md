# Sensor calibration methodology

Calibration of the conductivity probes was performed against a two-point
reference standard (84 µS/cm and 1413 µS/cm at 25 °C), with temperature
compensation coefficients derived via linear regression across the ambient
range observed in-situ (14.2–22.7 °C). Inter-unit variance post-calibration
was reduced to ±1.8%, deemed acceptable relative to the diurnal signal
amplitude. Probes exhibiting hysteresis beyond tolerance were excluded from
the deployment cohort (n=2 of 14).
