# Site selection rationale

Deployment sites were selected to maximize spatial coverage of the watershed
while satisfying accessibility and vandalism-risk constraints. A weighted
multi-criteria matrix (hydrological representativeness 0.4, access 0.3,
security 0.3) was applied to eleven candidate locations, yielding the six
operational sites. Site F was relocated 40 m upstream mid-deployment owing to
sediment accumulation compromising intake integrity.
